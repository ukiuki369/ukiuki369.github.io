AuraWall Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

This policy explains how the AuraWall Android app ("AuraWall", "the app", "we") handles information. AuraWall is published by ukiuki369. Contact: intongwonjiraporn@gmail.com.

1. Information the app itself collects

AuraWall does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, files or precise location. The wallpapers are generated on your device. The app stores a few settings only on your device (for example your chosen live wallpaper color, whether the daily wallpaper is on, and which premium wallpapers you unlocked). These settings never leave your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

AuraWall is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share with Google:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • IP address (which may be used to estimate general location)
  • Ad interactions (for example ad views and taps), app performance and diagnostic information
  • Basic device information such as device model, operating system version and language

Google uses this information as described in its policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Details of what the SDK collects: Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Ads appear only inside the app's own screens. The live wallpaper and the daily wallpaper feature never show ads.

3. Consent and your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button at the top of the app.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • You can stop all data collection by the app by uninstalling it.

4. Permissions

  • Set wallpaper: needed to apply the wallpaper you choose. It does not read your current wallpaper or photos.
  • Internet and network state: needed to load ads.

5. Children

AuraWall is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data security and retention

We do not run our own servers and do not store your personal information. Information collected by Google AdMob is kept by Google according to its own retention policies.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact

Questions or requests: intongwonjiraporn@gmail.com
